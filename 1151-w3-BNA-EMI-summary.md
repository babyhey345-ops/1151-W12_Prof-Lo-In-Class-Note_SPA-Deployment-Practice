## 1. Product Thinking (BNA Agent → Product)

**BNA Agent → Webpage → Service / SaaS**
- The agent's deliverable is a **webpage**, delivered as a **service** (SaaS).

**TP = Product** (software / app / application)
- A **Product** mediates between **Demand** and **Supply**.
- **Requirement** is split into **function** and **verification**.
- Development path: **MVP** (Minimum Viable Product) → **Commercialized**.
- The **Product** delivers **value (WTP, willingness to pay)** to the **TA (target audience)**.
- The Product goes to **Markets**, which consist of **Consumers** (and producers, per "e.g. Producers").

## 2. Analysis Process

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Separate components** (Σ parts) and identify their **relationships** |
| 2 | Break the topic into **the problem**, **the demand** and **the questions** |
| 3 | **Investigate deeply, with evidence** (e.g. from producers) |
| 4 | **Recombine** the parts into a **document** |

- **Tool:** Coggle mind map.
- **Result:** a **new representation → Insights**, which should be **systematic, structured and process-oriented**.

## 3. Evaluation Principle

- **Effective:** the goal or purpose is reached. **This is the criterion.**
- **Efficient** (output ↑ / input ↓) is crossed out, so it is **not** the primary criterion.
